# Xirius 1.1.0-beta.35

This beta is for Windows 10 and 11 (x64). To receive beta updates in the app, turn on **Receive beta versions** in Settings.

## Fixed

- On Windows, adding an ARA plug-in to a clip opens its editor automatically. In hands-on testing the editor opened in about two seconds.
- ARA pitch edits persist after you use Save As, close the song, and reopen it.
- The first Save As preserves ARA analysis even if you have made no edits in the plug-in.
- You can remove ARA from a clip and undo the removal to restore the processed audio.

These ARA workflows were verified by hand on Windows. macOS validation was limited to CI. ARA workflows with a real plug-in were untested on macOS.

## Known issues

- When you reopen a song, an ARA clip can keep showing a loading indicator until you click the clip.
- If an ARA editor goes behind another app, Xirius may be unable to bring it back to the front.
- Editing or removing ARA may leave the song marked as saved even when it has unsaved changes.

If the in-app update from 1.1.0-beta.2 or 1.1.0-beta.3 fails, close Xirius and use the installer on this page.
