# Xirius 1.1.0-beta.43

This beta is for Windows 10 and 11 (x64). To receive beta updates in the app, turn on **Receive beta versions** in Settings.

## New

- Drag the lower half of a clip's right edge to make the clip longer and fill it with repeats of its loop. A clip without a loop repeats all of what it was. The drag is one undo step. The pointer shows the left-right arrow with a loop sign, and Alt still moves where the loop starts. The upper half of the edge still changes the length.
- Looped clips show a loop mark among the marks at their top right.
- **Loop clip** in a clip's right-click menu (Ctrl+Alt+L) loops the selected clips, and is checked when they all loop. Choose it again to take the loop off. It is also available from the command line and to MCP clients (`clip.loop.set --toggle` and `--fill`).

## Changed

- When you drop an audio file on the arrangement, its ghost now stays in place until the clip appears, and the clip fades in where the ghost was. Before, the lane briefly closed and showed nothing.
- With the standard arrow, pressing the lower edge of a clip now selects and moves it instead of splitting it. Splitting from the lower edge is now only for the extended arrow (in Settings > Edit, set the select tool's note editing to Extended), which shows the scissors, a band and the cut line there. The Split tool, Alt+X and **Split clip** in the menu are unchanged.
- The small loop corner at a clip's bottom right is gone. Use the lower half of the right edge instead.

## Fixed

- A saved number, such as a plug-in's knob or a track's volume, could come back a tiny step off when the song was opened again. It now reads back exactly as it was saved.

## Known issues

- When you drag a file from File Explorer over a place that doesn't take files, the pointer still shows the copy sign. Nothing is placed if you let go there.
- Xirius may continue using CPU while the confirmation for closing a song or quitting is open. This is under investigation.

If the in-app update from 1.1.0-beta.2 or 1.1.0-beta.3 fails, close Xirius and use the installer on this page.
