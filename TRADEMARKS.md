# Trademarks

## The project's name and logo

"Xirius" and the Xirius logo (`assets/brand/`) identify this project. The licenses
(`MIT OR Apache-2.0`) cover the source code; they do not grant the right to use the name or the
logo as the name or logo of another product.

- You may say that your work is "based on Xirius" or "a fork of Xirius".
- A modified build or a fork distributed to others needs its own name and logo, and has to say
  that it is not the official Xirius.
- Unmodified official builds may be redistributed under the Xirius name.

## Names of other parties

Xirius does not use another party's trademark as its own name, logo, or product wording. In
particular, neither "VST" nor "ASIO" is part of the name of Xirius, of any of its builds, of its
logo, or of what the screens call the product.

- **VST** is a registered trademark of Steinberg Media Technologies GmbH. Xirius loads VST3
  plugins; it says so as a plain statement of what it supports ("VST3"), the way a file format
  is named. The VST logo is not used by default; if it is shown (the About screen, the web site,
  the documents), it follows Steinberg's usage guidelines. VST2 is not supported.
- **ASIO** is a trademark and software of Steinberg Media Technologies GmbH. ASIO output, where
  it is offered, is an optional feature. The ASIO SDK is not part of this repository; if the
  official binaries are built with it, that is under Steinberg's license, and a fork has to get
  its own agreement before it ships the ASIO SDK or its logo.

find the one you already know. The names belong to their owners:
